Updated 25 October 2026. This notice explains the two forms on the Habi Digital Group website.

Online enquiry form

When you submit the enquiry form, your name, company, email address, optional phone number, selected service, message, and follow-up consent are sent through FormSubmit to amparooskar@gmail.com. Habi uses these details to respond to your enquiry and discuss the services you requested. Submitting does not subscribe you to marketing.

FormSubmit processes the submission as an external email-delivery service. Its own website and privacy information explain its handling of data. Hosting and email providers also process normal technical and delivery information. Do not include passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal data.

Roadshow conversation brief

The separate roadshow brief downloads a text file on your device. It does not submit an enquiry or save form entries in browser storage. You choose whether to share that file. On a shared device, clear the form and protect downloaded files after each conversation.

Questions and requests

For questions about information you submitted, or to request access, correction, or deletion, contact amparooskar@gmail.com. Enquiry records should be retained only as needed for follow-up and any resulting business relationship, subject to applicable obligations.

Site resources

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and is delivered by its hosting provider. Those services may receive technical request information, including an IP address. No advertising pixels or analytics scripts have been added by Habi.
