05 / Good questions

Before we
get started.

Straight answers for your next conversation.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Start with the problem that matters most: a brochure, a landing page, a campaign, or your hosting setup. The scope can grow when there is a clear reason.

Do you guarantee rankings or sales?

No. Search rankings and commercial results depend on competition, demand, budget, your offer, and other factors. We agree on practical deliverables and measurement rather than promise an outcome we cannot control.

How much will a project cost?

Pricing depends on deliverables, complexity, quantities, and ongoing support. A useful brief lets us prepare a relevant scope. Advertising spend, print quantities, software subscriptions, and hosting terms should be clarified separately in a proposal.

Can you work with our existing brand or website?

We can begin by reviewing what you already have. Depending on access, platform limitations, and condition, the recommendation may be focused improvements, a migration, or a rebuild.

What should we bring to the first conversation?

Your website address, current marketing materials, main business challenge, preferred timeline, and any available performance reports. Never share account passwords in a brief.

What does SEO-ready mean?

It means a foundation that search engines and people can understand: meaningful page titles, descriptive headings, crawlable content, mobile layouts, sensible internal links, and structured information. Indexing and rankings require a public site and continued work; they are not guaranteed.
